Our commitments

Policies

The terms every order runs on — written down here so there are no surprises on either side.

Revisions

Every order includes 2 small revisions after delivery — adjustments such as color tone, wording, layout tweaks or file format. Larger changes that expand the original scope are quoted separately before we proceed, so you always approve any extra cost first.

Privacy & your files

Your photos, documents and recordings are used only for your order — never shared, resold, published, or used to train models. Source files are deleted after delivery and your confirmation, and finished work is never used for our own promotion without your permission.

Refunds

Changed your mind before work begins? You receive a full refund. If we find we cannot deliver what was quoted — for example, damage beyond what a preview sample showed — you also receive a full refund. Once work is delivered, any issue is settled by stage: we refund fairly for the stages not completed, as agreed with you.

Turnaround

Your delivery time is confirmed in writing with your fixed quote before you pay — most photo, transcription and document orders take a few days. Working to a deadline? Rush handling can usually be arranged when agreed in advance.

Good to know

FAQ

How is my order delivered?

Everything is delivered digitally — by email or a download link. There are no physical goods and no shipping; you receive print-ready or ready-to-present files you can use right away.

How long does a project take?

Most photo, transcription and document orders are finished within a few days; larger analysis, website and presentation projects depend on scope. We confirm the delivery time with your fixed quote, and rush service is available when you have a deadline.

How do I pay?

By PayPal. Once the scope and quote are confirmed, we send you a PayPal invoice or payment link, and work begins as soon as payment is confirmed.

What if I want changes after delivery?

Two small revisions are included with every order — tell us what to adjust and we will revise the files. Larger changes of scope are quoted separately before we proceed. See our Policies for details.

What happens to the photos and files I send you?

Your materials are used only for your order — never shared, resold, or used to train models. Source files are deleted after delivery and your confirmation, and finished work is never used for our own promotion without your permission.
